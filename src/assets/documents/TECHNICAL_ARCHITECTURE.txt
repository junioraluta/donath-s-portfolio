5. Data Architecture
We avoid hardcoding content inside components.
Example:
Instead of:
<h2>Business Development Officer</h2>
<p>Managed client onboarding...</p>
Use:
experience.js
Example:
export const experiences = [
 {
   company:
   "Emagination Power Consulting Ltd",

   role:
   "Business Development & Operations Officer",

   period:
   "Aug 2025 - Present",

   achievements:[
      "Managed business development activities",
      "Tracked sales pipeline",
      "Maintained 90%+ client retention"
   ]
 }
]
Benefits:
    • Easy CMS migration
    • Cleaner components
    • Easier updates
