
 INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
Website Philosophy
The website should tell a story.
Not
Here is my CV.
Instead
Here's who I am, the value I create, and why organizations trust me.
A visitor shouldn't have to "search" for information. Every section should naturally lead to the next.

User Journey
Every visitor follows a psychological journey.
Visitor lands

↓

Gets interested

↓

Learns about Donath

↓

Builds trust

↓

Sees evidence

↓

Feels confidence

↓

Makes contact
Every page supports that journey.

Mobile Strategy
Every section must be designed mobile-first.
Rules
    • Single column 
    • Large tap targets 
    • No horizontal scrolling 
    • Short paragraphs 
    • Cards stack vertically 
    • Timeline becomes vertical 
Desktop is simply an enhancement.
